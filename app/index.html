⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze) — meditate at every spot across Leonida. Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 counts toward it and disappears after Chapter 1. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Every other trophy can be earned after the credits.

Roadmap to the Platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

  1. Before you start: turn off cheats (they disable trophies and saving). Make a manual save at the start of Chapter 1.
  2. Stage 1 — Story, Chapters 1–4: play the campaign in order; clear meditation spots (incl. Lucia's balcony in Ch. 1), stranger missions and WAiNK spots in each newly opened area. Chapter trophies: Paradise Lost → T**s Ass Drug Money → Vice City, Baby! → Top Of The World, Ma.
  3. Stage 2 — Final mission: manual save first, then complete it for “You Gotta Know When to Get Off” (Gold). The open world stays available afterwards.
  4. Stage 3 — Side strands & activities: finish the Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret strands; 8 Strangers & Freaks strands (Andy, Billy and Tyrone do not count); races, drone races, shooting range, fishing, arcade, bowling.
  5. Stage 4 — Collectibles & grinds: 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK spots, 100 vehicles, all garages, 5 bank vaults (unlock via “That's Where The Money Is”, late Ch. 4), 1 illegal operation per gang, $250,000 laundered, 250 stunts, 50 Challenges.
  6. Stage 5 — 100% completion: the stricter checklist — 300 stunts, 70 of 80 Challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles, all 8 vaults, all 39 illegal operations, 42 shooting-range golds, all 14 Top Fun locations — earns “A Long Road” (Gold), then the Platinum pops.
